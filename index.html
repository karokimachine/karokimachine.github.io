Welcome

Welcome to my website!

I'm KarokiMachine, affectionately known as KM™, (or Kilometer…)

I’m a 15 year old game developer aiming for the stars. After practising making games with my first small project, execute>loop, I fell in love with the process, and I’m working on something huge for the future of KarokiMachine Games with a brilliant team.

In this page, you can find game links, information, my “timeline”, team members, and galleries of featured artworks from in-server challenges.

the game releases in parts, currently, part 1 is available to play.The game follows a mysterious, reserved internet man, and the strange, retro interface that comes with him.

CURE✚Yume-Chan♡

CURE✚Yume-Chan♡ is my second portfolio project, directed and produced by me and a small team.

The game follows a sickly, sensitive, insecure young girl named Yume who despserately wants to be admired.

After meeting the beautiful Magical Doctor in her dreams, things start to feel much better.

Timeline
  1. 06/11/26
    karokimachine.com was created!

    This is the date of karokimachine.com's creation.

  2. 3-9/9/26
    First Yume-Chan Design

    I had become inspired on a walk home after school while listening to Kikuo. What if someone were to be replaced by a perfect version of themselves?

    Later that day, on call with my dear friend Nin, we were coming up with stories, and that is when I found the basic plot of CURE✚Yume-Chan♡. Several days later, on the 9th, the first concept design was created!

  3. 1/9/26
    Part 1 releases

    Part 1 of execute>loop finally came out on this day, and hit 300 downloads almost immediately, alongside 100 Discord members. This was a major milestone for me.

  4. 6/4/26
    Project started

    As soon as I got home, I immediately got to work making the base. The basic RenPy textbox, simple sprite and background, and very few lines of dialogue were the start of execute>loop.

  5. 2-5/4/26
    Concept of execute>loop

    While on holiday, I started thinking about how to turn my original character into a game purely out of boredom. I started sketching sprites, backgrounds, and the very first posters on the plane home.
